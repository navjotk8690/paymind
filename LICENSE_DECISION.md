# License decision required

The code is intentionally delivered without a final public license choice.

The stated project goal is community access while discouraging paid proprietary resale. Those goals do not map perfectly to a standard OSI open-source license:

- AGPL-3.0 keeps hosted modifications open, but still permits commercial use.
- A non-commercial source-available license can restrict paid use, but is not OSI open source.
- Trademark protection can prevent third parties from presenting forks as official PayMind.

Choose a license with qualified legal advice before public release. Until then, do not assume third parties have permission to redistribute or sell the project.
